## Why this line

- **On subject:** a small good thing that happened today — a remembered coffee order and an unexpected free fried banana.
- **Warmth:** a tiny act of recognition from someone who sees you every morning.
- **Surprise:** the wink, and the feeling of a long-planned conspiracy over a banana.
- **Concreteness:** the warung lady, coffee with no sugar, the fried banana, this morning.
- **Fresh wording:** no greeting-card phrasing; it reads like something that actually happened.
